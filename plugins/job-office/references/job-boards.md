# Job Board Guide

Use this to suggest boards when the user has none, and to know how to work each one. Always ask the user which boards they want; this list is a starting point. Before relying on any board detail, check the live site, since boards change.

## How to classify a board (record in profile.md)

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Access | public search / login needed to apply / login needed to search |
| Automation | allowed (employer portals, ATS forms) / not allowed (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and similar aggregators) |
| Apply route | on site form / redirects to employer portal / email |

## Global and multi country

- LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Glassdoor (search and read only; user submits)
- Employer career sites and their ATS (Workday, SuccessFactors, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, Taleo): automation allowed with care
- Remote first: We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Working Nomads, Wellfound (startups)
- Creative and design: Behance Jobs, Dribbble Jobs, Contra, Working Not Working
- Tech: Stack Overflow style boards come and go; check GitHub's community job lists and company sites
- Nonprofit and development: Idealist, ReliefWeb, Devex, UN Careers, Impactpool

## By region (examples, verify current status)

- Canada: Job Bank (government), provincial health authority sites, Indeed Canada, Workopolis
- United States: USAJobs (federal), state job banks, Handshake (students), Dice (tech)
- United Kingdom: NHS Jobs, Civil Service Jobs, Reed, Totaljobs, CV Library, Guardian Jobs
- Ireland: Jobs.ie, IrishJobs, HSE careers
- Australia and New Zealand: Seek, APS Jobs, Trade Me Jobs
- Germany and EU: StepStone, Make it in Germany, EURES
- Gulf: Bayt, GulfTalent, NaukriGulf
- Nigeria and West Africa: Jobberman, MyJobMag, Hot Nigerian Jobs, LinkedIn
- Kenya and East Africa: BrighterMonday, Fuzu
- South Africa: PNet, CareerJunction
- India: Naukri, Foundit, Instahyre

## Sector specific

- Healthcare: national health service sites (NHS Jobs, provincial and state health authorities), hospital career pages, nursing registries
- Education: TES (UK and international schools), Schoolspring (US), university HR pages
- Academia and research: jobs.ac.uk, HigherEdJobs, EURAXESS, Nature Careers

## Search strategy per board

1. Use the role name plus two or three synonyms (for example "Health Care Aide", "Personal Support Worker", "Care Assistant", "Nursing Assistant").
2. Filter by location, contract type, and posted date when the board allows.
3. Sort by newest; record `last_checked` per board so the next scan only reports new items.
4. Open each promising posting to read requirements and closing date before scoring.
