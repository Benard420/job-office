# Job Scam Check

Run on every job before it is shortlisted. Mark `scam_flag` in jobs.csv as `none`, `caution`, or `high`, with a short reason in `fit_notes`.

## High risk (do not shortlist; tell the user why)

- Asks the candidate to pay anything: application, training, uniform, visa processing, "equipment" fees.
- Asks for bank details, ID numbers, or copies of passports before a formal offer and contract.
- Contact only by WhatsApp, Telegram, or a personal email (gmail, yahoo, outlook) for a company that claims to be large.
- Offers a job without an interview, or interviews only by chat.
- Sends a cheque or payment to "buy supplies" and asks for money back.
- Visa sponsorship promised for a fee, or "guaranteed" overseas jobs through an agent who charges the worker.
- Domain looks like a real company but is slightly misspelled.

## Caution (shortlist only with a note)

- Pay far above market for the role and location.
- Vague duties, no company name, no address.
- Very urgent language ("start tomorrow", "limited slots").
- Posting copied across many unrelated locations with identical text.
- Recruiter profile is new, has few connections, or no company page.

## How to verify

- Find the employer's own careers page and confirm the same posting exists there.
- Check that the email domain matches the company website.
- Search the company name with "scam" or "reviews".
- For overseas recruitment, check that the agency is licensed by the relevant government (for example, licensed recruitment agency registers in the destination country).

Tell the user plainly when something looks risky. Never apply to a high risk listing.
